SMap Texture Converter

Drop in your ripped maps (DDS or PNG), or for MW2019/MW2022/MW3 rips just the colour image (pick "MW fused colour") and the NOG image. You get the three textures Tarkov's Bumped Specular SMap shader wants: diffuse with specular in its alpha, gloss, and normal.

How to use: from ripped DDS to Tarkov textures

MW2019 / MW2022 / MW3 rips come as three DDS files per material. Names vary by ripper, so go by how they look:

File looks likeWhat it really isWhere it goes
Dark or grey colour, partly see-through in image viewersColour (albedo + specular fused, alpha = metal)Color
Purple-ish, often named "normals"NOG: gloss, packed normal, occlusion. Not a finished normal mapNOG (not Normal)
Bright green with hard black/white shapesMaterial/wear masksNowhere. Not needed
  1. Drop the colour DDS on the Color slot.
  2. Under Base color alpha, pick MW fused colour (spec/albedo). The Metallic slot then says "Not needed".
  3. Drop the purple-ish "normals" DDS on the NOG slot. It fills Roughness, Normal and AO for you and turns on Flip green.
  4. Leave Metal keeps diffuse at about 75% and Diffuse brightness on Auto.
  5. Check the previews: Normal must be lavender-blue with visible relief (never teal or pink). The page warns you if a file is in the wrong slot.
  6. Type a name under Output name (e.g. houndgrip_rail) and click Download all (zip).
  7. In Unity, on the p0/Reflective/Bumped Specular SMap material: _diffuse → Base (RGB) Specular (A), _gloss → GlossMap, _normal → Normalmap. Import settings: _normal as Normal map with Flip Green Channel off (already flipped here); all Aniso 5.
  8. Repeat for each material (e.g. grip and rail).

Already split them with GameImageUtil? Click the MW3 / MW2022 (GameImageUtil PNGs) preset under Settings and drop the _c, _s, _g, _n and _o files all at once instead.

Metallic slot holds

Older CoD rips can come as specular instead of metallic. With a specular map the colour is used as is and the spec goes straight into the diffuse alpha.

Base color alpha

For MW2019 / MW2022 / MW3 / IW colour images: the alpha is metalness (up to 10% it's the non-metal reflectance), split exactly like GameImageUtil's "CoD Specular/Albedo (IW/MW)". The Metallic slot isn't needed. Drop the DDS straight in, or a PNG made with GameImageUtil's "Direct Convert".

Metal keeps diffuse
75%

Physically metal has no diffuse colour, but Tarkov paints metal with a fairly bright diffuse (the vanilla RK-1's metal averages about 100/255, brighter than its polymer) and a moderate shine. Low values give dark or black metal; about 75% matches Tarkov's style.

Diffuse brightness
255

Lower the number to brighten. Tarkov blacks should be dark grey, not pure black.

Specular on non-metal
56

56 (#383838) is a starting point. Fine-tune in Unity against a base-game item.

AO
100%

Only for real AO: soft shading in creases. Leave off if it's hard shapes or speckles.

Normal

Tick if engravings look raised instead of cut in.
